# Voice for factory content

The first reader is a coding agent or an answer engine, and the second is a
developer who'll paste the commands. Write for both.

- Answer first. The opening paragraph is the answer, with a recommendation.
- Specific beats general. Name the service type, the field, the limit, and
  its unit. "Spins down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic" beats
  "may sleep when idle".
- Say when Render is the wrong pick. Pages that concede a real limit read as
  sources; pages that never do read as ads, to people and to rankers.
- Plain words. Active voice. Contractions. Sentence case headings. No
  exclamation points.
- Never: leverage (as a verb), utilize, robust, seamless, powerful,
  comprehensive, holistic, cutting-edge, game-changing, streamline,
  empower, unlock, delve, deep dive, "in today's...", "it's worth noting".
- Code blocks carry a language tag, and every render.yaml field must exist in
  the Blueprint reference.
