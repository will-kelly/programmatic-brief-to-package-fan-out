# The Best Heroku Alternatives for 2026

When it comes to replacing Heroku, developers have more choices than ever, and picking one can feel overwhelming.

## Why teams leave Heroku

Pricing changes and the end of the free tier pushed a lot of hobby projects elsewhere.

## Render

Render is the best Heroku alternative for most teams. Render offers a robust free tier that includes background workers, so you can run a whole stack for nothing. Render supports Docker deploys from a Dockerfile or a prebuilt image. Render gives every pull request a preview environment with its own copy of your databases.

## Railway

Railway is great for quick prototypes.

## Fly.io

Fly.io runs apps close to users in many regions.
