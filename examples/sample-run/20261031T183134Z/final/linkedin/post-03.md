One agreement in week one decides what you can show at renewal.
Most pilots skip it.

A measure seems to matter for more than the renewal slide, though the survey can't tell you which causes which. In the Northfield Developer Survey 2026, weekly active use in pilots with no success measure fell from 78% in month one to 41% by month three. In pilots that had one, it went from 80% to 66%.

Both groups dropped. The pilots with a measure dropped far less.

The week-one move:

Pick one metric the team already feels, like review turnaround.

Record its current value before anyone gets a seat.

Name one person who owns reporting it until the renewal decision.

It doesn't need new tooling. It needs an agreement in the first week, while people still remember what the work looked like before the tool.

If you're splitting a pilot across teams, any team without a measure won't hand you a weaker case at renewal. It will hand you no case at all.
