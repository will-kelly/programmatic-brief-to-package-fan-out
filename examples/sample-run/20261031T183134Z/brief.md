# Research brief: why AI coding assistant pilots stall at day 90

## Thesis

Most AI coding assistant pilots don't fail on model quality. They stall around
day 90 because the organisation measured seat activation instead of changed
work, so when the renewal decision arrives there's no evidence either way.

## Audience

Engineering directors and platform leads at companies with 200 to 2,000
developers who've run, or are about to run, a coding assistant pilot.

## Evidence

- [E1] In the Northfield Developer Survey 2026 (n=1,240 developers, fielded
  March 2026), 61% said their company's assistant pilot had "no agreed success
  measure" at launch.
- [E2] The same survey found weekly active use fell from 78% in month one to
  41% by month three among pilots with no success measure, and from 80% to 66%
  among pilots that had one.
- [E3] Halvorsen Research's 2026 platform team report (32 companies,
  interviews) found the median pilot ran 94 days before a renewal decision.
- [E4] Of the 32 companies in [E3], 9 tracked any change in cycle time or
  review load during the pilot. All 9 renewed. Of the 23 that tracked only
  seat activation, 11 renewed.
- [E5] Counterpoint: a 2025 Ostrava University study of 400 pull requests
  found no measurable change in review time with assistant use, which suggests
  some teams are right to stall.

## Anecdotes

- [A1] A platform lead at a logistics company described the day-90 meeting:
  the CFO asked what had changed, and the only slide was a seat-activation
  chart at 92%.
- [A2] A director at a fintech ran a two-team pilot where one team agreed in
  week one to track review turnaround. That team's numbers made the renewal
  case; the other team's didn't exist.
- [A3] An engineering manager kept a paper log of "things I'd have searched
  for" each day for three weeks and found most assistant use replaced
  documentation lookups, not code writing.
- [A4] (shared) The author's own experience: a pilot they advised in 2024
  where nobody owned the measurement plan until the week the budget was cut.
